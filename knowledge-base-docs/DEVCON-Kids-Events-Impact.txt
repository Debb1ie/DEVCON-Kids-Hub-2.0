# DEVCON Kids — Events and Impact

## Impact Figures

IMPORTANT: These figures cover different periods and different metrics. They must never be summed, and each must be quoted with its period.

### Calendar Year 2025 (published July 2026)
- Students reached: 3,825
- Volunteers activated: 180
- Teachers trained: 100+
- Events executed: 29
- Schools reached: 32

### Combined 2022 and 2023 (as published on devconkids.org)
- Scholarships: 1,407
- Code Camps: 35
- Lead Learners trained: 212
- Special Events: 1,574 [VERIFY — this may represent attendees at special events, not count of events]
- Hour of Code students: 958

### Cumulative Since 2018
More than 10,000 children reached — stated by Pam Ros Damarillo in June 2026.

### Parent Organisation Context (DEVCON Philippines, for comparison)
- 19,000 geeks face-to-face nationwide in 2025
- Led by Bukidnon, Iloilo, and Manila chapters
- Roughly 1 million aggregated online reach annually
- Community of over 70,000

## Timeline of Key Milestones

- 2009: DEVCON Philippines founded
- 2018: DEVCON Kids launched. Cagayan de Oro becomes the first chapter outside Metro Manila
- 2019: Robotics sessions with partner schools (robot-sumo with Mano Amiga, 14 Feb 2019)
- 2020: Pandemic period; Scratch teacher account maintained for remote learning
- 2021: micro:bit lending programme with CSTA Philippines
- Oct 2022: Los Angeles USA chapter established
- Feb 2023: Cebu chapter activated
- 2023: Manila chapter established. LA Discovery Expo (165 attendees)
- 2024: Coding Herstory launched with American Spaces Manila. Hour of Code nationwide celebration
- 2025: Hour of AI rolls out nationwide. Largest documented single-year impact
- Mar 2026: Sui Foundation "Build Beyond" partnership launched
- Jun 2026: PromptWars AI hackathon with Tutorials Dojo. DEVCON 17th anniversary
- Jul 2026: Hour of AI 2026 sessions begin; advanced AI hardware touring nationwide

## Child Protection Policy

DEVCON Philippines maintains a Child Protection Policy written specifically around DEVCON Kids. Full text: https://devcon.ph/child-protection-policy/

Scope: Applies to all staff, volunteers, board members, contractors, partners, interns, and anyone else working with children.

Guiding principles:
- Paramountcy of the child — the child's best interests are the primary consideration
- Prevention — a culture of prevention with safeguarding measures
- Protection — from all forms of harm including physical, emotional, sexual abuse, neglect, exploitation
- Participation — children's right to express views
- Equality and inclusion
- Accountability and transparency

Reporting: Concerns or suspicions must be reported immediately to the designated Child Protection Officer(s) at DEVCON Philippines.

If you have a child safety concern, contact DEVCON Philippines' Child Protection Officer via devcon.ph/child-protection-policy and local authorities as appropriate.
