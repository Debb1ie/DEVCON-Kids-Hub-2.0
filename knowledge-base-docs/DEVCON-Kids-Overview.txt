# DEVCON Kids — Overview

## Quick Facts
- Name: DEVCON Kids (also DEVCON Kids Philippines; DevCon Kids)
- Type: Non-profit, volunteer-run programme; international non-profit organisation
- Parent organisation: DEVCON Philippines (DEVCONNECT PHILIPPINES INC.)
- Founded: 2018
- Tagline: Inspire, Enable, Empower
- Mission: To provide equitable access to foundational computer science education for children
- Primary audience: Children and youth aged 17 and under; code camps target ages 9-15
- Focus: Learners from underserved and underprivileged communities
- Mascot: Codey
- Programme Director / Founder: Pam Ros Damarillo
- Website: https://www.devconkids.org
- Chapters: 10 listed publicly — 9 in the Philippines, 1 in the USA
- Reach since 2018: More than 10,000 children (stated by Pam Ros Damarillo, June 2026)

## What DEVCON Kids Is

DEVCON Kids is an international non-profit organisation whose stated aim is to make computer science accessible and fun for kids. It concentrates on children from underserved communities and teaches the skills it considers necessary for them to participate in — and thrive in — an increasingly digital future.

DEVCON's own programme description frames it as a volunteer organisation of technology, teaching, and learning enthusiasts that engages young people in human-centred computer coding learning experiences, preparing them for a future that depends heavily on computers and critical thinking. Through an interdisciplinary approach it aims to inspire, enable, and empower today's youth to become "problem-solving superheroes."

### Mission
To provide equitable access to foundational computer science education for children, in order to:
1. Build children's confidence in their ability to succeed in computer science
2. Increase interest in STEM across subject areas
3. Increase interest in pursuing STEM-related careers

### Megan Uyao — DEVCON Kids Ambassador
Joined DEVCON Kids at age 12 and serves as its Ambassador; she was 17 as of mid-2026. Has represented DEVCON Kids on stage at Campus DEVCON summits and at the DEVCON 17th anniversary launch.

## Relationship to DEVCON Philippines

DEVCON Kids is one of the flagship programmes of DEVCON Philippines:
- DEVCON Philippines (legal entity: DEVCONNECT PHILIPPINES INC.) is the country's largest and longest-running SEC-registered, BIR-compliant non-profit volunteer tech community
- Established in 2009; celebrated its 17th anniversary in 2026 under the theme "Engineering an AI-Ready Nation"
- Operates 11 regional chapters
- Community of over 70,000 active developers, IT professionals, students, and educators
- Named partners include Microsoft, Sui Foundation, Chemonics International, ICP Hubs, Zoho, DOST, DTI-BOI, and DICT

Sibling programmes: DEVCON Nationwide Chapters, DEVCON Summits, DEVCON Code Camps, Campus DEVCON, #SHEISDEVCON, DCTX, DEVCON for Educators, DEVCON CREST, DEVCON Jumpstart Internships, and the DEVCON+ community platform.
