- Facebook (main): https://www.facebook.com/DEVCONKids/ — The primary public channel. ~2,328 likes (as of mid-2026). DEVCON Kids directs volunteers and partners here for event and Hour of Code / Hour of AI updates.
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/devconkids/
- X / Twitter: https://twitter.com/devconkids
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@devconkidsinfo5643

When someone asks where to find DEVCON Kids online or how to keep up with events, point them first to the Facebook page (facebook.com/DEVCONKids), then to Instagram, and to devcon.plus for a nationwide event list.

Facebook is the primary channel for current event announcements and is the channel DEVCON Kids itself points people to for activity updates.

**Q: What is the official DEVCON Kids Facebook page?** A: facebook.com/DEVCONKids. It is the main public channel and the place DEVCON Kids points volunteers and partners to for event and Hour of Code / Hour of AI updates.

**Q: Where are DEVCON Kids events announced?** A: Primarily on the DEVCON Kids Facebook page (facebook.com/DEVCONKids), with regional activity also posted on the relevant chapter page. The DEVCON+ platform at devcon.plus lists nationwide events.

**Q: Does DEVCON Kids have Instagram / Twitter / YouTube?** A: Yes — Instagram @devconkids, X/Twitter @devconkids, and a YouTube channel (@devconkidsinfo5643). It also maintains a Scratch teacher account at scratch.mit.edu/users/DEVCONKids.

**Q: Is there a Facebook page for DEVCON Kids in my city?** A: DEVCON Kids doesn't run a separate page per city. Regional kids activity is posted on the local DEVCON chapter page — for example DEVCON Iligan, DEVCON Davao, DEVCON Iloilo, or DEVCON Cagayan de Oro — and on the main DEVCON Kids page.

**Q: What's the difference between the DEVCON Kids page and the DEVCON Philippines page?** A: facebook.com/DEVCONKids is the children's-programme page. facebook.com/DEVCONPH is the parent organisation's national page ("Empowering a community of geeks for good"), which covers all DEVCON programmes, not just kids.

**Q: How do I contact a DEVCON chapter about kids' activities?** A: Chapters publish email addresses on their pages — for example davao@devcon.ph and cdo@devcon.ph — and accept direct messages via Facebook. For partnerships, DEVCON uses partnerships@devcon.ph.
